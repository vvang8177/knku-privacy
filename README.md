KNKU Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

KNKU (“we”, “our”) provides a family tree app.

Information we collect
• Family tree content you enter (names, dates, relationships, and related details)
• Photos you add to profiles
• Custom events you create
• Basic app preferences (theme and similar settings)

How we use information
• To build and display your family tree
• To save profiles, photos, and events
• To sync and back up data when cloud sync is enabled
• To operate and improve the app

Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. Data is stored on your device and on our service providers (such as Supabase) to run sync and storage.

Data retention
We keep information as long as needed to operate the app. You may delete content in the app, clear app data, or contact us to request deletion of cloud copies.

Children
KNKU is not directed to children under 13.
