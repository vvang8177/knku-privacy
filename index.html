KNKU Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026 · App: KNKU (com.knku.app)

KNKU (“the App”) is a family tree app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Family tree content. Information you enter about people in your tree, such as names, dates, places, relationship details, notes, education, occupation, and similar life details.
  • Photos. Profile pictures you choose from your camera or photo library.
  • Events. Custom family events you create in the App (birthdays may also be calculated from dates you enter).
  • App preferences. Settings stored on your device, such as theme or display preferences.
  • Technical data. Basic service logs needed to operate and secure our backend (for example, request timing or errors) when cloud sync is used.
  • Push notification tokens. If you allow notifications, a device push token may be stored so we can send event alerts when the App is closed (via Apple/Google push services and our cloud backend).

We do not require email/password accounts to use the core family tree features. The App does not show ads.

2. How we use information

  • Build and display your family tree in the App
  • Save person profiles, photos, and events
  • Sync and back up tree data across devices when cloud sync is enabled
  • Support share features you choose to use (for example Share Tree)
  • Operate and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where information is stored

Tree data, events, and preferences are stored on your device. When internet is available, the App may also store tree data, events, and photos using Supabase (cloud database and file storage) so the family tree can sync across devices that use this App configuration. Data may be processed on servers in regions used by our providers.

4. Who can see your information

  • You can see and edit the family tree content on devices where the App is installed and synced.
  • Other devices using the same shared tree configuration may receive the same synced tree data, photos, and events.
  • Service providers (such as Supabase) process data only to host and operate the App infrastructure.
  • If you use share features, content you choose to share leaves the App according to the share target you pick.

5. Photos and user-generated content

If you upload photos of yourself or others, you must have the right to share them. Do not upload content that is illegal, exploitative, or otherwise prohibited. Enter information about family members only when you are authorized to keep those family records.

6. Permissions

  • Camera — to take a profile photo if you choose.
  • Photo library (system picker) — to pick an existing photo if you choose. On Android this uses the system Photo Picker without broad access to your entire media library.
  • Notifications — if you allow them, for app-related alerts.
  • Internet — to sync data and photos when cloud sync is used.

You can deny permissions; some features (like photos or sync) may not work without them.

7. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the App. Cloud copies remain while sync is used for that family tree. We may retain limited records for security, abuse prevention, or legal reasons.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • You can edit or delete people, photos, and events in the App.
  • You can clear app data or uninstall the App to remove local copies.
  • To request deletion of cloud-stored tree data or photos, contact us at the email below and include enough detail for us to find your records (for example, approximate dates you used the App and what should be removed).

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
